SHAHI TUKDA

Shahi tukda is a bread pudding eaten in Pakistan and India, and it's particularly famous in Delhi, Lucknow, and Hyderabad.

INGREDIENTS LIST
  • bread slieces
  • sugar
  • water or rose water
  • ghee or oil
  • cardamom powder
  • saffron threads
  • full cream milk
  • heavy cream
  • milkmaid
  • nuts and dried fruits
INSTRUCTIONS:
  1. Step 1: Prepare Sugar Syrup
    1. Combine sugar and water in a saucepan.
    2. Heat until sugar dissolves, then bring to a boil.
    3. Reduce heat and simmer for 5-7 minutes or until syrup thickens slightly.
    4. Add cardamom powder and saffron threads (if using). Set aside.
  2. Step 2:Fry Bread Slices
    1. Heat ghee or oil in a pan.
    2. Fry bread slices until golden brown on both sides.
    3. Drain excess oil on paper towels.
  3. Step 3:Prepare Creamy Mixture
    1. Boil full cream milk in a saucepan until it reduces slightly.
    2. Add heavy cream and stir well.
    3. Add Milkmaid and mix until well combined.
    4. Simmer for 5-7 minutes or until mixture thickens slightly.
  4. Step 4: Assemble Shahi Tukda
    1. Dip fried bread slices in sugar syrup for a few seconds.
    2. Place bread slices in a serving dish.
    3. Pour creamy milk mixture over bread slices.
    4. Garnish with chopped nuts or dried fruits.
